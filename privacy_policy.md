# Exhale – Privacy Policy

_Last updated: October 13, 2026_

Exhale ("the app") is a breath-controlled musical instrument for Android, developed and published by **Three Stars Interactive** ("we"), which is the developer and data controller for the app. This policy explains what the app does with your information. The short version is that **Exhale does not collect, store or share any personal data.**

## Microphone
Exhale asks for the microphone permission so that you can play the instrument by blowing into your phone.

- The microphone signal is processed **live, in memory, on your device**, only to measure how loudly you are blowing (a loudness level).
- **No audio is recorded, saved to storage, or transmitted.** The app itself has no internet permission, so it cannot send anything off your device.
- The microphone is only used while you are on the play screen with the app in the foreground.
- You can decline the permission and play in "breathless" mode (a Hold to blow button) instead. You can change your choice at any time in Android Settings › Apps › Exhale › Permissions.

## Data we collect
None. Exhale has:
- no accounts or sign-in,
- no advertising and no advertising IDs,
- no analytics, crash reporting or tracking SDKs,
- no internet access of its own (the INTERNET permission is not requested; optional purchases are handled by the Google Play Store app, see below).

## In-app purchase (Exhale Plus)
Everything currently in Exhale is free. Exhale offers one optional, one-time in-app purchase, "Exhale Plus".

- Purchases are processed entirely by **Google Play**. Your payment details, name, email and billing address are handled by Google under the [Google Play Terms of Service](https://play.google.com/about/play-terms/) and the [Google Privacy Policy](https://policies.google.com/privacy); we never see or receive them.
- Through the Google Play Billing Library, the app receives only the **status** of the purchase (for example purchased, pending or refunded) and a purchase token that Google Play uses to confirm it. The app stores a flag on your device recording whether Exhale Plus is unlocked, so it works offline.
- We do not run a server and do not collect or keep a purchase history. Google keeps your order history in your Google account, and you can see it in the Play Store.

## Data stored on your device
Your app settings (for example instrument, sound, background, tuning and ambience choices, and whether Exhale Plus is unlocked) are stored locally on your device. If Android backup is enabled on your device, these settings may be included in your Google account's device backup, which Google handles under its own privacy policy. Exhale itself never receives them.

## Children
Exhale does not collect personal information from anyone, including children.

## Third-party content
The app includes recorded instrument samples, ambience recordings and typefaces licensed from third parties. These are bundled inside the app and do not involve any data sharing. See Settings › About in the app.
